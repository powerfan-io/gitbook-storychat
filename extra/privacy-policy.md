# Privacy Policy

Storychat Privacy Policy\
Effective Date: June 13, 2025

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how PowerFan Inc. d/b/a Storychat (“Storychat,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information about you when you access or use any of our websites, mobile applications, browser extensions, APIs, content‑creation tools, or any other products or services that link to this Policy (collectively, the “Services”). Please read this Policy carefully. By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

Undefined capitalized terms have the meanings assigned to them in the Storychat Terms of Service.



1. Information We Collect

***

The categories of information we collect depend on how you interact with the Services.

**1.1 Information You Provide Directly**

* **Account Details** – Name, email address, password or social‑login token, preferred language, and any profile information you choose to add.
* **Payment Information** – Limited payment card details or digital‑wallet address collected and processed by our third‑party payment processors (we do not store full card numbers).
* **User‑Generated Content** – Chat messages, uploaded images or audio, character descriptions, stories, comments, and any other content you create or share through the Services.
* **Communications** – Inquiries, feedback, survey responses, bug reports, or other information you send to us.

Providing certain information is optional, but some features will not work without it.



**1.2 Information We Collect Automatically**

When you use the Services, we—and in some cases our analytics or advertising partners—automatically collect:

* **Log and Device Data** – IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring/exit pages, click‑stream data, and timestamps.
* **Usage Data** – Features you use, pages viewed, interactions with content, session duration, scroll depth, error reports, and performance metrics.
* **Approximate Location** – Derived from your IP address.
* **Cookies and Similar Technologies** – Small files placed on your device that allow us to recognize your browser, store preferences, conduct analytics, detect abuse, and personalize content. See Section 6 (Cookies & Similar Technologies).



**1.3 Information from Third Parties**

* **Social Logins** – If you sign in with Google, Apple, Discord, or similar services, we receive information such as your name, email address, and social‑media identifier, as permitted by your settings with that service.
* **Referrals and Integrations** – Information supplied by other users who invite you, partners who run joint campaigns with us, or third‑party platforms you connect to your Storychat account.
* **Publicly Available Sources** – We may supplement your profile with publicly available information to detect fraud or enforce community rules.

3. How We Share Information

***

We disclose information only as described below:

* **Service Providers.** Vendors who perform services for us (e.g., cloud hosting, customer support, data storage, payment processing, analytics, and security) may access information strictly to carry out their work under confidentiality agreements.
* **Analytics & Tracking Partners.** We use Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, and similar tools to understand user behavior. These partners place cookies or similar technologies in order to collect usage information and provide us with aggregated statistics, session replays, and heat‑maps. Microsoft’s terms are available at [https://clarity.microsoft.com/terms](https://clarity.microsoft.com/terms).
* **Affiliates.** Subsidiaries and corporate affiliates may access information consistent with this Policy.
* **Legal or Safety Reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law or in good‑faith belief that such action is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Storychat, our users, or the public.
* **Business Transfers.** Information may be transferred as part of, or during negotiations of, a merger, sale of assets, financing, or acquisition of all or part of our business.
* **Public or User‑Directed Sharing.** Content you make public (e.g., public characters, stories, group chats) and actions you take within shared spaces are visible to others. You may also direct us to share information with third parties of your choosing.
* **With Consent.** We may share information with unaffiliated parties when you give us permission to do so.

We do **not** sell personal information for monetary consideration, and we do not knowingly allow targeted advertising to children.



4. Your Rights & Choices

***

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

* Access, correct, or delete personal information
* Portability of certain information
* Object to or restrict certain processing
* Opt out of direct marketing or targeted advertising
* Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing)
* Appeal a decision we make regarding your request

To exercise any of these rights, please email [**privacy@storychat.app**](mailto:privacy@storychat.app) or use the in‑app request form. We may verify your identity before responding.

You may also manage cookies (Section 6) and unsubscribe from marketing emails via the links in any message.

Residents of California, Nevada, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions with special laws can find additional disclosures in the Regional Supplements at the end of this Policy.



5. Data Retention

***

We retain information only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Policy, to comply with our legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize personal information within a commercially reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or where public characters or content you created has become integral to other users’ experience (e.g., highly popular public characters).



6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

***

We and our partners use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and session‑replay tools to:

* Keep you logged in
* Remember preferences
* Record session interactions (scrolling, clicks, navigation paths) via Microsoft Clarity to help us improve usability
* Measure traffic and usage patterns
* Detect and prevent fraud

Most browsers accept cookies by default. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the Services may not function properly. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt‑out Browser Add‑on. Microsoft Clarity respects the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where legally required.



7. Security

***

We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—such as encryption in transit and at rest, role‑based access controls, and regular security audits—to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password and authentication credentials confidential.



8. International Data Transfers

***

Storychat is headquartered in the United States. We may transfer and store information outside of the country where you live, including to the U.S. and other jurisdictions that may have data‑protection laws that differ from yours. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved safeguards to protect cross‑border transfers of personal data.



9. Children’s Privacy

***

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it as required by law.
