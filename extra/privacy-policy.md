# Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy**

Effective Date: June 26, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how PowerFan Inc. d/b/a Storychat ("Storychat," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you access or use our services (the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

***

**1. Information We Collect**

We collect the following types of information:

* **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your email, username, and password.
* **User Content**: Any characters, stories, images, messages, or media you generate or upload.
* **Usage Data**: Interactions with the platform (e.g., page views, time spent, scrolls, clicks), session replays, device type, operating system, and browser.
* **Payment Information**: Payment method details are processed securely by our third-party payment provider (e.g., Stripe).
* **Cookies & Tracking Data**: See Section 6 for details.

***

**2. How We Use Your Information**

We use the information we collect to:

* Provide and improve the Services
* Process payments and manage subscriptions
* Personalize your experience
* Respond to customer inquiries
* Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
* Analyze usage trends via tools like Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics

***

**3. How We Share Information**

We may share information with:

* **Service Providers**: Vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, customer support, data storage, analytics, security, payment processing) under confidentiality agreements.
* **Analytics & Tracking Partners**: We use Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, and similar tools to understand user behavior through cookies, session replays, and heatmaps. Microsoft’s terms: [https://clarity.microsoft.com/terms](https://clarity.microsoft.com/terms)
* **Affiliates**: Our subsidiaries and corporate affiliates, consistent with this Policy.
* **Legal or Safety Requirements**: If required by law or to protect Storychat, our users, or the public.
* **Business Transfers**: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.
* **User-Directed Sharing**: When you create or interact with public content, such as characters, stories, or group chats.
* **With Consent**: When you authorize us to share data with third parties.

**Note:** We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration and do not knowingly allow targeted advertising to children.

***

**4. Your Rights & Choices**

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

* Access, correct, or delete your personal information
* Request portability of certain data
* Object to or restrict specific types of processing
* Opt out of direct marketing or targeted advertising
* Withdraw consent at any time
* Appeal a decision regarding your data request

To exercise these rights, email [privacy@storychat.app](mailto:privacy@storychat.app) or use the in-app request form. We may verify your identity before responding.

You can also manage cookie preferences (see Section 6) and unsubscribe from marketing emails using links in our messages.

***

**5. Data Retention**

We retain information as long as necessary to:

* Provide the Services
* Comply with legal obligations
* Resolve disputes
* Enforce agreements

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize personal information within a commercially reasonable period, unless:

* Retention is required by law
* The content you've created (e.g., popular public characters) has become integral to the experience of others

***

**6. Cookies & Similar Technologies**

We and our partners use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and session replay tools to:

* Keep users logged in
* Save preferences
* Record interaction behaviors (e.g., scrolling, clicks)
* Improve platform usability and design via Microsoft Clarity
* Measure traffic and usage
* Detect fraud and security threats

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some Services may not work properly. You may opt out of Google Analytics using the [Opt-out Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Microsoft Clarity honors Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where required by law.

***

**7. Security**

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including:

* Encryption (in transit and at rest)
* Role-based access controls
* Regular security audits

However, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password and credentials.

***

**8. International Data Transfers**

Storychat is headquartered in the United States. We may store or process data in the U.S. or other jurisdictions. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful safeguards to protect data transferred across borders.

***

**9. Children’s Privacy**

The Services are not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect information from such users. If we become aware that a child has submitted personal data, we will delete it as required by law.

You must be at least 13 years old to use our Services (16 in the EEA/UK).

***

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective Date" above and notify you of material changes via the Services, email, or other reasonable methods. Continued use of the Services after such changes means you accept the updated terms.
