# Response Length 설정하기

Control how long your Character replies are—short and snappy, or long and immersive.\


In any chat, click the “Response Length” option from the sidebar.

Choose a value from 1 to 10:

* Lower = shorter replies
* Higher = longer replies

#### 💸 Usage & Access

* Levels 1–3 → Free for everyone
* Levels 4–10 → Costs 10 SP per reply
* Silver & Gold users → Unlimited access to all levels, no SP required
