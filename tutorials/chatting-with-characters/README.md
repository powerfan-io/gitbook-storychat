# Chatting with Characters

Storychat lets you chat freely with thousands of AI characters created by users around the world.

**💡 Switch to Smarter AI Models**

Want higher-quality replies and better memory?

Upgrade to a premium AI model by clicking the icon in the bottom-left corner of the chat.

**✨ Describe Thoughts and Actions**

**🪄 Don’t Know What to Say?**

Tap the wand icon for 3 smart suggestions from AI.

Use them as-is or tweak them to match your style.
