# Feed – Discover, Interact, and Earn

### 📰 Feed – Discover, Interact, and Earn

The Feed is where your Series and Storychats are showcased to the world.

Series have higher visibility than individual Storychats, making them more likely to be discovered.

***

#### 📌 How to Navigate the Feed

* Swipe left/right → Move between different Series.
* Scroll down → Read the first Storychat in the Series.
* Scroll down again at the end → Continue to the next Storychat in the Series.

***

#### ❤️ Interact with Content

* Like → Show appreciation for the story.
*   Comment → Share your thoughts or feedback.

    Likes and comments are instantly sent to the creator, helping them understand audience reactions and improve future content.

***

#### 💰 Monetize in the Feed

One of Storychat’s most unique features—earn from sharing content you love.

1. Find a Series or Storychat you enjoy.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Your unique public link is generated.
4. When others join Storychat through your link:
   * Your invite code is applied automatically.
   * You earn 30% of all their payments for 1 year.\

💡 Important:

Revenue goes to the link sharer, not the original content creator.

Creators benefit by gaining free global promotion, helping them grow faster than on any other platform.
