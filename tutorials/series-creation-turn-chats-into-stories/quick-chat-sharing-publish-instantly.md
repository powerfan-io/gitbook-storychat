# Quick Chat Sharing – Publish Instantly

### ⚡ Quick Chat Sharing – Publish Instantly



Don’t have time to build a full Series?

You can still share your favorite Storychats with just one click.

***

#### 📌 What It Does

Publishing a chat instantly posts it to the Feed, so other users can read and enjoy it—no Series required.

***

#### 🛠 How to Publish a Chat

1. Inside your chat, open the side menu.
2. Click Publish Chat.
3. Your Storychat will:
   * Appear in the Feed for all users to see.
   * Lock the chat so no further edits can be made.

***

#### ✏️ Editing After Publishing

To make changes:

1. Click Unlock to unpublish the chat.
2. Edit as needed.
3. Publish again when ready.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-08-05 at 3.36.44 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 🔗 Share Your Published Chat

1. Once published, click the Share button.
2. Copy your unique link and send it anywhere—Discord, Twitter, Instagram, etc.
3. Anyone clicking your link can view your chat instantly.
