# Create a Series

### 🎬 Create a Series – Share Bigger Stories

Want to boost your content’s visibility and grow a loyal fanbase?

Publishing as a Series puts your work at the top of the Feed and makes it easier for readers to follow your story.

***

#### 🌟 Why Create a Series?

* Higher visibility in Feed and Explore.
* Group related Storychats into a single storyline.
* Build a dedicated audience for long-term engagement.
* Maximize monetization with referral links.

***

### 🛠 Two Ways to Create a Series

#### 1️⃣ From the Create  Page

1. Go to Create → tap Create Series.
2. Add:
   * Thumbnail (cover image)
   * Title
   * Description
   * Category
   *   Hashtags\


       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-08-05 at 3.53.38 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. Toggle Make Public Series to allow other users to discover it on Explore.
4. Tap Next to go to the Storychats tab.
5. Tap + Add Storychat:
   * Choose a Character first.
   *   Select one or more Storychats from that Character.\


       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-08-05 at 3.53.55 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6. You can:
   * Add or remove Storychats anytime.
   *   Use Edit Order to arrange episodes.

       _(The first Storychat in the list will be Episode 1.)_

***

#### 2️⃣ Directly from a Chat

1. While chatting, if you find a great moment, open the side menu and tap Publish Chat.
2. When prompted, choose:
   * New Series → Create a new one from scratch.
   *   Add to Existing → Add this chat to a series you’ve already made.\


       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-08-05 at 3.52.59 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. You’ll be taken to the Series editor with your current Storychat automatically included.

***

### 📢 After Publishing

* Public Series get priority placement in Feed and Explore.
* Readers can binge your stories like on Netflix or Wattpad.
* The Series profile shows:
  * Series info
  * All included Storychats
  *   Easy navigation between episodes\


      <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-08-05 at 4.10.28 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### 💰 Monetize Your Series

* On the Series profile, tap Share to generate a referral link.
* Share it anywhere—social media, forums, communities.
* When someone joins Storychat through your link:
  * Your invite code is applied automatically.
  * You earn 30% of all their payments for 1 year.
